If you are a student, postdoc or junior faculty working on statistics, data science, or related fields consider applying to present a poster.


## Application {#application}

Please send us an email to `mj.junca20@uniandes.edu.co` with subject `Poster Statistics and Data Science Workshop` including the title and abstract of your paper (and a link to the paper if there is one online).
